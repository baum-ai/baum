## The type: columns

2 or 3 labelled lists side by side, up to 8 items each: before and after,
can and can't, kept and removed. A column has a `label` (at most 40
characters) and a `tone`; an item has a `title` (at most 80 characters) and
an optional `note` (at most 80). Items in the same row of two columns
should correspond, so the reader compares across.
