## The type: table

2 to 5 `columns` (headings of at most 40 characters) and up to 12 `rows`
of the same length. A cell is a string of at most 120 characters, or, when
the table compares before and after, `{ text, was }`: `text` is what it is
now and `was` what it was before this change (each at most 120
characters); the page shows `was` struck through beside it. Use a table for
values a reader compares (limits, codes, states, who may do what, what
happens in each case), never for prose. The page folds a table after 8
rows, so put the rows that matter most first.
